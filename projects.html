Counter-Strike Player Performance Prediction

My final year project focuses on predictive analysis of Counter-Strike player statistics. The aim was to use historical player and match data to predict whether a player is likely to perform well in their next game, producing a result that is both technically interesting and understandable to esports fans.

The project gave me practical experience with data cleaning, feature engineering, model selection, evaluation and explaining model performance in a real domain rather than a generic classroom dataset.
